---
title: 'The Apocalypse'
---

On December 18 2025 the Apocalypse hit P4nth3rworld!
After many months of neglect, the toxic and radioactive material in the swamp piled up and got out of control.
All the items became one, creating a massive black hole, swallowing all of the items, the entire world, and all of its inhabitants.

Only a void remains now. Will there ever be life here again, for now the answer is no.
